Access & authentication

  • No passwords are stored. Sign-in is by one-time email link or Google OAuth, so there is no password database to breach.
  • Row-level security is enforced in the database on every table holding personal data — a rider can only read and write their own records, enforced by the database itself rather than by application code.
  • Administrative rights are held in a protected field that users cannot alter, guarded by a database trigger.
  • Secrets are held in the hosting platform's encrypted environment store, scoped to server-side execution only, and never committed to source control.
  • TLS on every connection, with HSTS enabled.
  • Least privilege: the key that can bypass access rules is used by exactly one server-side function (the payment webhook) and is never available to the browser.

Payment data

Card details never reach our systems. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider. We hold only a Stripe customer reference and the status of a subscription. We cannot see, store or export a card number — and neither can anyone who compromised us.

Data we hold

  • Email address (required — it is how people sign in)
  • Optional profile: display name, home town, country, riding type, ability, short bio, photo
  • Contributions: reviews, ratings, posts, saved towns, trip plans, routes
  • Membership status and a Stripe customer reference

No tracking or advertising cookies. No analytics profiling. No sale of personal information, ever.

Where it lives

Data is held with our providers, which operate infrastructure in Australia, the United States and the European Union. This is disclosed in our privacy policy, as Australian Privacy Principle 8 requires.

Partner data — your listing, your contact details, your published offers — is yours. It is exportable on request and deleted on request.

Backups & recovery

  • Daily automated database backups, retained 7 days, held by our database provider
  • Weekly independent backup to storage we control, retained 90 days — so a failure of the provider is not a failure of the backups
  • Every backup is verified as restorable at the point it is taken
  • Deployments are versioned; any previous version of the site can be republished in under a minute

Monitoring & response

  • Automated availability checks every five minutes against a health endpoint that verifies the database, payments and mapping services independently
  • Application error tracking with alerting
  • Faults alert a named person by email and SMS
  • A documented operations runbook covers restore, rollback and incident response

Editorial integrity

Relevant to any publicly funded partner: paid placement is always labelled as paid, and payment does not affect a ranking or a rider rating. Rankings come from rider ratings and published editorial criteria. We do not publish invented reviews, invented statistics or fabricated bylines.

We remove fake, abusive or competitor-written reviews — not merely unflattering ones.

What we don't have yet

Stated plainly, because you will ask and we would rather you heard it from us:

  • No third-party penetration test has been commissioned. We use automated dependency and configuration scanning.
  • No ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification.
  • No 24/7 staffed operations desk — monitoring is automated and alerts a person.
  • We are a small team. Continuity depends on documentation and shared credential custody, both of which are in place.

Where a contract requires an independent test or a specific certification, tell us early and we will scope it.
