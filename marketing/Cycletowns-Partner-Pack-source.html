The market

Cycle tourism is big,
and growing fast.

Cycling holidays stopped being a niche a decade ago. Riders travel further, stay longer and spend more than the average visitor — and they choose where to go based on the riding, not the marketing.

Figures from Cycletowns' market analysis, consistent with published industry research valuing the global market at US$146–153bn in 2025 and forecasting 9–11% annual growth to 2033.

💰
High-yield visitors
International cycling trips average around A$9,000. For a Member partner, one extra booking a month typically covers the tier several times over. Cyclists also travel mid-week and in the shoulder season — exactly when a regional business needs the trade.
📅
They plan, and they plan early
A cycling trip is researched over weeks: which region, which roads, which stays, which café at the 60km mark. That long planning window is a long window to be found in — if there's somewhere to be found.

The gap we're filling

Strava, Komoot and AllTrails map rides. TripAdvisor reviews everything, for everyone. None of them is built for the businesses that want cyclists — there is no dedicated place for a bike-friendly town, stay, shop or operator to be discovered by the riders planning to travel. Cyclists currently plan across a dozen fragmented sources: forums, heatmaps, blogs, word of mouth.

Cycletowns is the supply-side platform for cycle tourism. We exist to make the places and operators findable — and we monetise that side, not the rider's attention.

Where we're starting

We're building region by region rather than thinly everywhere. The beachhead is Bright and Victoria's High Country — owned properly, with real depth, then replicated. Founding partners in a beachhead region get built into the guide first and shape how their region is presented.

Cycletowns is a Sport2040 project, drawing on 30+ years of relationships across events, content and audience in Australian sport.

Straight talk on the flywheel

This is a two-sided market, and we're honest about which side comes first. Partners come before the audience. Your listings, routes and local knowledge are what makes a town guide worth reading — and a guide worth reading is what brings the riders.

That's why founding pricing exists. You're taking on some of the early risk with us, and the rate you join at reflects that, permanently.

"Why not wait until you're bigger?"

Fair question, and one we get asked. Three answers: founding partners get the lowest rate, held for as long as they stay; they get top placement in a region while there's room at the top; and the guide for their town gets built with them rather than around them. First movers own the region.

Straight answers

Does paying improve my ranking?
No. Paid placement is labelled as paid. Rankings come from rider ratings and our editorial scoring, and money doesn't touch either.

Can I remove a bad review?
No. We remove reviews that break the rules — fake, abusive, or written by a competitor — but not ones that are simply unflattering. You can reply to any of them.

What if it doesn't work?
Cancel from your dashboard. You keep the rest of the month you've paid for, and your free listing stays.
